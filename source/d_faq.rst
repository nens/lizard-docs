==========================
Frequently Asked Questions
==========================
	
This section will be extended in the near future. 


How do I get access to an organisation?
---------------------------------------

Before rights were granted through the servicedesk. 
However, we have opted for a new system where the organisations themself grant rights to users.
This way they are totally aware and in control of who has access to their data.

To be able to access the data of an organisation you have to ask permission to one of the organisations Managers.
These have the ability to give you the permissions required.


How do I export a raster?
--------------------------

Raster exports are available from within the `Lizard Viewer <b_viewer>`_ or the `Lizard Catalogue <b_catalogue#Exporting>`_. 


How can I inspect my rasters?
---------------------------------------

If you visit your Lizard portal, you can continue to the data management section. You will find two options there: the raster sources and raster layers. Raster sources are objects with the raw data. Raster layers are visualisations of the data within one or more raster sources. 

By navigating to the raster sources, or layers, you will find a list of all the raster sources or layers you have access to. You can click on the name of a raster source or layer to view its details, including metadata, statistics, and visualisations. Within this detail page, at the bottom, you will find the 'ACTIONS' button. This button provides the option to delete a raster source or layer.


How can I switch between organisations?
---------------------------------------

In the viewer it is possible to switch between organisations by clicking on the organisation name in the top right corner of the screen. If you have permissions for multiple organisations, you will see a dropdown menu with the names of the organisations you have access to. By clicking on the name of the organisation, you can switch to that organisation and view its data.
